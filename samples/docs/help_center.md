# Orbit Help Center

## Refund policy
You can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Changing your plan
1. Open Settings and choose Billing.
2. Click Change plan and pick a new tier.
3. Confirm the change; the new price applies from the next billing date.

## Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication is an extra security step that asks for a code from your phone when you sign in. You can enable it under Settings > Security.

## Data export
Admins can export all workspace data as a ZIP file from Settings > Data. Exports are ready within 24 hours and the download link expires after 7 days.

## Supported browsers
Orbit works on the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. Internet Explorer is not supported.

## How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and follow the emailed link. The link is valid for 1 hour.

API keys are credentials that let scripts access your workspace. Treat them like passwords and rotate them every 90 days.
